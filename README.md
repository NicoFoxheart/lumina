# LUMINA ✨

**Light in darkness**

An intimate, premium interactive experience about pain, beauty, and the courage to keep going.

## About

Lumina is a web-based 3D visualizer that explores the coexistence of pain and beauty through an intimate, touching narrative. Through interaction, users transform tears into stars and watch broken glass glow from within, discovering that they themselves are the light they've been searching for.

## Experience

The journey transforms through five stages:
- 🌑 **Darkness** - Heavy, cold, real
- 💫 **Awakening** - First touch of light
- 🦋 **Transformation** - Tears become stars
- 🌸 **Bloom** - Colors emerge
- 🌅 **Sunset** - Breathtaking finale

### Interaction

- **Move** your mouse/finger to create light
- **Click/tap** to create bursts of stars  
- **Watch** tears transform into light
- **See** broken glass catch rainbow colors
- **Feel** intimate messages speak to your soul
- **Experience** a stunning sunset transformation

## Features

- 400+ interactive particles with glowing trails
- 50 shattered glass fragments with rainbow refraction
- Dynamic color journey (darkness → sunset)
- Intimate, touching narrative messages
- Multi-layer particle glow effects
- Star twinkle and pulse animations
- Premium visual polish
- Fully responsive (desktop & mobile)
- Touch and gesture support
